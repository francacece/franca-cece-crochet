Fashion with a personal touch.

Franca Cece Crochet is a handmade brand for women who like their style to feel individual. Every piece begins as a single strand and is worked, stitch by stitch, into something you can wear and carry.

We take a modern approach to crochet: clean shapes, considered color and details that feel thought through. Because each piece is made by hand, it carries a little of the person who made it, and no two are ever exactly the same.

Whether you choose something from the collection or ask us to make something new, we want it to feel like yours.

Making things thoughtfully

We're working toward more responsible ways of making: choosing materials with care, reducing waste where we can, and exploring recycled and eco-friendly yarn options as the brand grows. It's a journey, and we'll keep it honest along the way.
